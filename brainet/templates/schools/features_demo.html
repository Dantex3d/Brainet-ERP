🚀 Go to Dashboard 📚 Try Promotion System
🏫 School-Wide Promotion Demo
Scenario: End of academic year - promote all 800 students in one click!
  1. Click "🎓 Student Promotion Center"

    Navigate from DOS Dashboard

  2. Select "🏫 Promote Entire School"

    Shows: 8 classes, 800 students

  3. Review Summary

    Grade 10 (300) → Grade 11, Grade 11 (350) → Grade 12, Grade 12 (150) → Graduated

  4. Confirm Promotion

    Click "🚀 Start School-Wide Promotion"

  5. Done! ✓

    All promotions completed. View in history. New classes auto-created.

📚 Class-Specific Promotion Demo
Scenario: Promote Grade 10 East (120 students) only
  1. Go to Promotion Center

    Dashboard → 🎓 Student Promotion Center

  2. Select "📚 Promote by Class"

    List shows all classes with student counts

  3. Click "Grade 10 East"

    Shows 120 students to be promoted

  4. Review Students

    See all names, admission numbers, current stream

  5. Confirm Promotion

    120 students → Grade 11 East (auto-created if needed)

👤 Individual Student Management Demo
Scenario: Handle special cases - promote, repeat, or drop individual students
  1. Search for Student

    Use search box on Promotion Center

  2. Click Student Name

    Shows: John Doe, Grade 10 East, ADM#12345

  3. Choose Action:
    • ✓ Promote → Grade 11 East
    • 🔄 Repeat → Stay in Grade 10 (low marks?)
    • ✕ Drop → Remove from school (transferred?)
  4. Add Remarks

    Optional: "Excellent performance" or "Transferred to ABC School"

  5. Submit

    Action recorded in history with timestamp and who did it
