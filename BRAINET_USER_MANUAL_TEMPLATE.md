# Brainet User Manual & Account Activation Templates

## 1. Welcome Email Templates

### Teacher

**Subject:** Brainet Account Verified — Teacher Access Ready

Hello **[Teacher Name]**,

Your Brainet account is now verified and active.

You can access:
- `Teacher Dashboard`
- `Class lists`
- `Student accounts`
- `Assignments`
- `Exam tracking`
- `Fee statements`

**What to do next**
1. Login to Brainet.
2. Open `Teacher Dashboard`.
3. Review assigned classes.
4. Check student accounts for payment balances and term fees.
5. Use `Assignments` and `Exams` to manage work.

If you need help, contact your school administrator.

## 4. Student Accounts and Fees Note

- Students are excluded from the onboarding email distribution.
- Use the role templates for `Teacher`, `DOS`, `Principal`, and `Bursar` only.
- The system tracks student payments and balances in `Student Accounts`.
- Fees should be wired into student accounts with correct term allocation and overpayment handling.
